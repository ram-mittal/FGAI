# Brag Plan: AIM3002P Generative AI Course Portal

## What is this app?
A clean, modern, responsive university learning portal for the "Fundamentals of Generative Artificial Intelligence" course, consolidating modules, labs, projects, and resources in one place.

## The angle
A sleek, academic-but-modern "unboxing" of the course portal. The joke/hook is that finding course material is finally simple and beautiful (finding things in 2-3 clicks), contrasting with typical clunky university LMS systems.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The AIM3002P course badge sliding in, followed instantly by the bold title "Fundamentals of Generative Artificial Intelligence."

## Key moments (the middle)
- The gradient avatar of "Prof. Shraddha Jadhav" popping in as the Course Instructor.
- The colorful "Quick Access" cards (Modules, Labs, Projects) cascading onto the screen.
- The large statistical counters for Modules and Resources ticking up rapidly.

## Outro / punchline
"Built with heart for students. Made by Ram Mittal."

## User flow worth showing
1. Landing page hero section
2. Clicking/highlighting the "Quick Access" Lab or Project cards
3. Showing the collaboration links and resources available.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: Sleek academic showcase
- Interpretation: Fewer scenes, longer holds. Confidence through restraint, letting the deep blue academic palette and clean typography shine.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 18 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: oklch(0.99 0.002 260) (Light Mode Deep Blue Academic)
- Accent: oklch(0.42 0.15 260) (Primary Color)
- Text: oklch(0.17 0.02 260) (Foreground Color)
- Display font: sans
- Body font: sans
- Strongest visual element: The colorful quick access icon cards and the violet-to-indigo gradient instructor badge.

## Share copy (draft)
"No more digging through clunky LMS portals. Built a clean, modern learning hub for AIM3002P: Fundamentals of Generative AI. Everything you need in 3 clicks. 🚀"

## Audio direction
- Role: sparse professional accents
- Music: upbeat-but-restrained lofi or chill electronic
- Music treatment: fade-in fast, drop to bed volume, fade under final logo
- Music cue guidance: 1 strong cue for the Quick Access cascade
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle glow on the primary badge
- SFX posture: moderate; clean clicks and swooshes for UI cards
- Audio-coupled moments: count-up for stats, card sequence cascade
- Restraint rule: No overly chaotic or meme-y sound effects.
